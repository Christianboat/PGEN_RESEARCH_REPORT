Comprehensive Analysis

The answers in the application responses show that, overall academic performance is average, with the preferred subjects of many of the students not being reflected in their career choices. To give an example, among students who made a choice in favor of the profession of Medicine, Social Studies or Arts were noted as their preferred subject, and among future engineers, no STEM-related subject was most likely to be preferred.

This misalignment suggests a guidance gap — students may not fully understand how subject strengths link to their dream careers. Additionally:

  • 67% female participation shows strong inclusivity, but few females selected STEM fields.
  • 42% subject-career alignment means more than half of students need mentorship on pathways.
  • Healthcare dominates as a career choice (51%), followed by Education and Engineering, suggesting high interest in traditional careers.
  • Public Speaking and Digital Skills emerged as the most requested skills, showing demand for 21st-century competencies.

Mentorship sessions should therefore bridge the alignment gap by linking subjects to real careers, and by equipping students with practical skills that complement their academic strengths.
